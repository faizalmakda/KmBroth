Why bone broth

A quiet daily ritual your body notices.

For generations, broth was medicine you could sip. As bones simmer, they release collagen, gelatine, amino acids and minerals into the water — the good stuff that's hard to get anywhere else. Here's what people reach for it for.

01

Gut health & digestion

Gelatine and glutamine help soothe and support the gut lining — the reason broth has long been the first thing offered to someone unwell.

02

Collagen for skin & hair

A natural, whole-food source of collagen and the building blocks your body uses for firmer skin, stronger nails and healthy hair.

03

Joints & mobility

Glucosamine, chondroitin and collagen — the same compounds found in joint supplements, delivered the way nature intended.

04

Immune support

Rich in minerals and amino acids to help you feel your best, especially through the colder Manchester months.

05

Naturally protein-rich

A warming, savoury way to add protein to your day — as a morning sip, a cooking base, or a nourishing broth on its own.

06

Clean & simple

Just bones, water and time. No stock cubes, no powders, no preservatives — nothing you wouldn't make in your own kitchen.
