Why bone broth

For centuries, broth was the first food offered to anyone who needed building back up.

As bones simmer low and slow, they release collagen, gelatine, amino acids and minerals into the water — nourishment that's hard to get any other way. Here's what people sip it for.

Gut health & digestion

Gelatine and glutamine help soothe and support the gut lining — the reason broth has always been comfort food when you're run down.

Collagen for skin & hair

A natural, whole-food source of collagen and the building blocks your body uses for firmer skin, stronger nails and healthy hair.

Joints & mobility

Glucosamine, chondroitin and collagen — the same compounds found in joint supplements, the way nature intended.

Everyday immune support

Rich in minerals and amino acids to help you feel your best, especially through the colder months.

Naturally protein-rich

A warming, savoury way to add protein to your day — a morning sip, a cooking base, or a nourishing broth on its own.

Clean & simple

Just bones, water and time. No stock cubes, no powders, no preservatives — nothing you wouldn't use in your own kitchen.
